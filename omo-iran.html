ReTargeting Iran by David Barsamian with Noam Chomsky, Ervand Abrahamian, Nader Hashemi, Azadeh Moaveni, and Trita Parsi A follow-up to Targeting Iran, this book updates the discussion with contemporary perspectives on diplomacy, de-escalation, and the failure of the nuclear deal.
