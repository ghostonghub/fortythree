  • Mamdani’s platform centers on affordability, equity, and

transformative urban policy. Key proposals include making all city buses free by 2027, funded by raising the corporate tax rate from 7.25% to 11.5% and imposing a 2% surcharge on individuals earning over $1 million annually—measures projected to generate up to $9.4 billion annually He also advocates for a rent freeze on all rent-stabilized apartments, the creation of a Social Housing Development Agency to build 200,000 new affordable units, and the establishment of one municipally owned grocery store in each borough to improve access to healthy, affordable food His plan includes expanding free school meal programs to city colleges and launching a universal childcare program for children aged six weeks to five years, estimated to cost $6 billion annually

toward a new Department of Community Safety staffed with mental health professionals and crisis responders His stance on Israel’s war on Gaza has drawn significant attention and controversy. Sanders, Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez, and the Working Families Party, and mobilized over 22,000 volunteers His victory, which saw record voter turnout, is seen as a significant moment for the Democratic Party and progressive politics Despite opposition from business leaders and criticism from former Governor Cuomo and President Donald Trump—who has labeled him a communist and threatened to revoke his citizenship—Mamdani has maintained a focus on policy and community engagement
