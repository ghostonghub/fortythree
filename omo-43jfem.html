Key Reports and Studies
  • Global Media Monitoring Project (GMMP) 2020 Report: Titled Who Makes The News, this report documented a sharp decline in female participation, noting women form only 13% of print reporters and 20% of radio newscasters in India, despite being 52% of TV news reporters.
  • UN Women Report (2021): Gender Inequality in Indian Media revealed that women occupied barely 5% of leadership positions in newsrooms from 2018 to 2019. It also noted that 85% of panelists on English debate shows were men.
  • RSF 2026 World Press Freedom Index: Specifically noted that women make up less than 15% of guests on major evening talk shows and highlighted the severe online harassment, including doxxing and death threats, faced by female journalists.
  • Academic Studies: Various papers such as Gender and Media: Status of Women Journalist in Hindi Print Media in India and The Fine Print: India's leading English dailies: A feminist study of the gendered practice of journalism analyze the systemic under-representation and objectification of women in the field.
