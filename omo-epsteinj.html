Q

Hardeep Puri Daughter Epstein

A
  • Himayani Puri, daughter of Union Minister Hardeep Singh Puri, is at the center of controversy due to alleged financial ties to individuals connected with Jeffrey Epstein.
  • Realm Partners LLC: Himayani Puri is linked to Realm Partners LLC, a New York-based investment firm. Reports indicate it received approximately $280 million (₹2,400 crore) in funding from Robert Millard, a businessman described as close to Epstein.
  • Unclaimed Property Notice: In April 2022, Massachusetts Mutual Life Insurance Company (MassMutual) issued a legal notice to Himayani Puri regarding unclaimed property, which has sparked speculation about the source and nature of the funds. The notice was repeated in 2023, 2024, and 2025, with no claim made by her as of February 2026.
  • Alleged Epstein Connection: While no direct evidence confirms her association with Epstein, social media and investigative reports have linked her to Epstein’s network through Robert Millard and others. Some sources also suggest her father’s connections may have facilitated these links.
  • Property Holdings: She is reported to have purchased a Manhattan apartment in 2013, allegedly at a below-market price, and owns property in Gurugram, India, raising questions about the source of funds.
  • Official Denials: Hardeep Singh Puri has denied any wrongdoing, stating his interactions with Epstein were professional, and that his daughter’s financial activities are unrelated to Epstein. No official proof has been presented to substantiate the allegations.
